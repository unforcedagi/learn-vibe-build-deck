An artifact about your final project

Part 1: your pod demo (2 minutes)

Post a link to your pod's demo from class on the class board. One thing together: one person posts it for the pod. Different things: everyone posts their own.

Part 2: the artifact (the real homework)

Make something that explains the project: what you're making and why. It isn't a piece of the project. It's an artifact about the project. Any form a podmate can take in in a few minutes.

On Nov 2 we'll form final-project pods from these artifacts.

Your artifact should cover

  1. What you're planning to make
  2. Who it's for, and what it makes better
  3. What the finished thing will look like
  4. Why this one
  5. Skills you're growing
  6. Where you're starting from
  7. What support you need, and solo with pod support or build together

Option: think it through with an AI. Your prompt is on the Week 9 page.
