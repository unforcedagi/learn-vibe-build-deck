Week 8 · Alive?3 / 21

1 · What is alive?

Three marks of a living system

Self-making

Autopoiesis: it produces and repairs its own parts, inside a boundary it makes itself. AI: not yet

Energy flowing through

A dissipative structure: it keeps its form by a constant flow of energy, like a flame or a whirlpool. AI: yes, but it's the data center

Knowing its world

Cognition: it navigates the world by sensing and responding. Even a bacterium. AI: increasingly, yes

Alive? Not yet. But it is getting harder to say why not. Where is its membrane? Must life be biological?

Maturana & Varela, Autopoiesis and Cognition 1980 · Prigogine (Nobel 1977) · Capra & Luisi, The Systems View of Life 2014

1 · What is alive?

Consciousness: cognition that turns back on itself

Feeling

There is something it is like to be you. Nagel 1974 · AI: unknown, no test

Access

Information available to the whole mind, to use and report. Block, Baars, Dehaene · AI: a lot of it

Reflection

Awareness of its own experience, and of itself. Edelman, Capra · AI: a sliver (about 1 in 5)

In every animal we know, feeling comes first and reflection last. AI may be showing reflection before anyone can find feeling.

1 · What is alive?

Ladder or ground?

A ladder

Consciousness grows: it emerges when cognition gets complex enough, integrated, modeling itself.

Most of neuroscience. If so, AI might climb it.

The ground

Consciousness is already here: the open awareness experience happens in. Complexity shapes what is known, not that anything is.

Contemplative traditions; some philosophers. If so: does AI share in it at all?

Last week we sat and just noticed. That awareness was simple, and we found it by doing less.

Perhaps it's more than these definitions.

Week 8 · Building together19 / 21

Building together · tips

Six habits for building as a pod

1 · One shared context

A README or brief that every member's AI reads. Same goal, same words.

2 · One home

A shared repo or doc, linked from the pod board ("My pod" tab).

3 · Split by pieces

Not by person-hours. Each piece has an owner.

4 · Midweek check-in

10 minutes, async. One message: what I tried, what's next, what I need.

5 · Integrate early

A rough link by Thursday beats a perfect one on Monday.

6 · Plan the demo now

3 minutes: what it is, how you split it, one thing that broke, what collaborating was like.

Now: ~50 minutes of build time. Leave with something started.
