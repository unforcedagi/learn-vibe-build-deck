Demo day

Why it matters

Over these two nights everyone shows what they built this semester. Treat it as a dry run for your capstone, and, if you want to turn your project into a venture, as practice for showing it to people outside class. Guests may come to watch.

What to bring

  • Something working that people can see. Small is fine; it should run on the night.
  • The story of how you built it: where you started and what changed.
  • One moment that surprised you.

Timing

Each slot is 10 minutes: about 6 to show and 4 for questions. Slots run in order from the start of class, so the time beside a slot is roughly when that person is up.

Before the night

Test your demo on the room’s screen setup: plug in, check that your screen and sound come through, and keep a backup (a short video or screenshots) in case something fails.
