A studio course about becoming a builder with AI. You make something every week, write about what you did in your own words, and share it with the room. The tools will keep changing. The point of this class is learning how to keep up with them.

The weekly rhythm

1. Make something. Any medium. Small and unfinished is fine.

2. Write at least one paragraph, yourself, about what you did and what you learned.

3. Share it. On Sep 14 everyone demos; after that, half the room demos each week and the other half gives real feedback.

Weekly work is due Sundays at 11:59 PM in Canvas. Canvas is the official home for submissions, grades, and announcements.
