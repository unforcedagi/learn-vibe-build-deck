Week 3 Everyone demos, then the tools.

ATLS 4519 · Sep 14, 2026 - Learn, Vibe, Build

Demos first.

Tonight - 1. Demos. Four minutes each. Timer on screen. - 2. A short tools talk from me. - 3. Small groups. - 4. Work time.

Why we demo.

Before the demos - This class is about building. - Building means sharing it and taking feedback. - Sharing is how you learn. You get witnessed. - Every week, everyone shares. No build? Share anyway.

Four minutes each.

Demos - Show the thing. - Say which tool you used. - Give us one surprise, or one place you got stuck. - Unfinished is fine. - Didn’t build? Share anyway. Your perspective counts.

What’s new this week

  • Sept 3: a whole fly brain, mapped. 166,700 neurons, 125 million synapses.
  • The internet ran it: Doom, Minecraft, Beat Saber, Bad Apple.
  • Real wiring, toy neurons, hand-picked inputs and outputs. Not a fly playing games.
  • The serious result: the wiring alone predicts behavior. No training.
  • The RL link: use the wiring as the shape of a learner, then let it learn.

It is moving fast. Stay balanced.

How to hold it - Every demo tonight is a sign of how fast this is moving. - Anxiety about the pace makes it harder to navigate. - Be aware of the rate of change. Do not clench around it. - Your relationship with technology matters more than ever. - howtounclench.com — Jonny Miller. Worth reading, and worth noticing: someone built it.

Three things vary.

The landscape - OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI. Plus open-weight models like Qwen and gpt-oss that anyone can download. - A chat window, a code editor, an agent in your terminal, or a builder in a browser tab. - Someone else’s cloud, or your own laptop. - Almost every tool you have heard of is one choice on each of these three.

Not free this term.

Know before you go - Nothing in this class requires you to pay for a tool.

Use three different tools.

Week 3 assignment - Start early. Free tiers cap you per day, so three tools does not fit in one night. - This is a suggestion, not a requirement. Show your learning about the tools another way if you’d rather. Experimentation and diversity are the point.

Three questions.

Small groups · 10 minutes - Which tool did you use for Week 2, and why that one? - What did it get right on the first try, and where did it fight you? - What would you try next, and what is stopping you?

Work time.

  • Get set up with at least one new tool tonight.
