Five tools follow, one slide each. The point is not comparison shopping — it is picking and starting.
Tool 1 of 5

Claude Code

What
An agent in your terminal: it reads your files, writes code, runs it, and fixes its own mistakes.
Costs
Needs a paid Claude plan — Pro is $20/mo (or $17/mo billed annually). No free tier for Claude Code itself.
Best when
You want the strongest coding agent and you’re willing to live in a terminal.
This is what builds the class site. Free claude.ai chat exists, but Claude Code the agent needs Pro or an API key.
Tool 2 of 5

Codex

What
OpenAI’s coding agent — in ChatGPT, in a CLI, and in your editor. Describe the app; it builds it.
Costs
A free taste on any ChatGPT account; real sessions need Plus, $20/mo. ChatGPT Edu is free with your CU login (short required training first).
Best when
You already live in ChatGPT and want your chat and your coding agent in one place.
CU's ChatGPT Edu went live Aug 14 — free for enrolled students after a ~30-min required AI training. OpenAI lists Edu among the plans that can sign into Codex; have someone with Edu access test it live. Week-1 promised claiming the $100 Codex student credits together — do that here if the offer still stands tonight.
Tool 3 of 5

Antigravity

What
Google’s agentic editor — a full IDE where Gemini-powered agents plan and build across editor, terminal, and browser.
Costs
Free with a Google account. And US students get a free year of Google AI Pro — claim by Dec 31 at gemini.google/students.
Best when
You want a real agentic tool for zero dollars.
Antigravity's free tier is a weekly-refreshed quota; the student AI Pro year raises those limits. The offer needs student verification and a payment method on file — tell them to set a reminder to cancel before renewal ($19.99/mo after). Antigravity needs Chrome installed for its browser agent.
Tool 4 of 5

Cursor

What
A code editor with AI built in — you see and touch the code while AI writes most of it.
Costs
Free hobby tier to start; Pro is $20/mo. The free student year closed to new sign-ups in June.
Best when
You want to learn what the code actually is, not just what it does.
Good on-ramp for students who felt uneasy about "not seeing the code" in week-1 feedback.
Tool 5 of 5

Grok

What
xAI’s chat — fast, current, good at images, with real free access on grok.com and X.
Costs
Free tier at grok.com and on X; SuperGrok is $30/mo if you want more.
Best when
You want a capable free chat companion next to whichever builder you pick.
Position Grok as a companion, not the main builder for tonight. SuperGrok Lite exists at $10/mo if anyone asks about a cheaper step up.
