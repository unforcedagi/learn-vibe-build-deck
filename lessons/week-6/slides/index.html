Week 6 · Demo 1 · Context that sticks5 / 19

What goes in AGENTS.md

  • Who it's for, and what they need
  • What it is, in one sentence
  • Stack and conventions ("plain HTML, no build step")
  • Where the facts live (and "never invent facts")
  • What "done" looks like
  • Keep it short, true, and secret-free

Week 6 · Demo 1 · Context that sticks9 / 19

Your link has to work on someone else's phone

✗  file:///Users/you/Desktop/project/index.html
     only works on your laptop
 
✓  https://you.github.io/project/
     works for anyone, anywhere

If it starts with file://, nobody else can see it.

Week 6 · Demo 2 · Get it online12 / 19

When the link doesn't work

  • 404: wait 1–2 min, then check Settings → Pages
  • Page with no styles: paths start with / (use style.css)
  • Only the README shows: index.html must be in the top folder
  • Old version: hard refresh, or wait a minute
  • Private repo on a free plan: make it public, or use Vercel
