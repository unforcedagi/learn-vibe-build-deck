## 2. This week's work: an artifact about your final project

**Due Sun Nov 1, 11:59 PM** · weekly submission, 10 points · submit on Canvas or through your LVB account

### Part 1: your pod demo (2 minutes)

Post a link to your pod's demo from class on the **class board**. If your pod made one thing together, one person posts it for the pod. If each of you made something different, everyone posts their own. We just want all of them in one place.

### Part 2: the artifact (the real homework)

For the rest of the term you'll grow one final project. This week, before you build more, make something that **explains** the project: what you're making and why. It isn't a piece of the project. It's an artifact *about* the project.

On **Nov 2** we'll form final-project pods from these artifacts, so tell us what will help us put you with the right people.

#### Your artifact should cover

1. **What you're planning to make.** One sentence, then as much detail as you need.
2. **Who it's for, and what it makes better.**
3. **What the finished thing will look like.** Describe it, sketch it or mock it up. What happens when someone uses it?
4. **Why this one.** Why does it matter to you?
5. **Skills you're growing.** What you want to be better at by December.
6. **Where you're starting from.** What you've built or learned this semester that this grows out of.
7. **What support you need.** Skills you're missing, questions you're stuck on, kinds of people you'd like in your pod, and whether you'd rather build solo with pod support or build together with others.

#### What form it takes

You choose: a page (the course site, your own site or a shared doc), a paragraph or two with artifacts (sketches, screenshots, links, a voice memo, a short video), or something you made (a zine, a diagram, a storyboard, a one-minute pitch). Make it something a podmate can take in in a few minutes.

#### Option: let an AI interview you

1. Gather your context: copy your earlier weekly submissions, reflections or project links (from Canvas, or your *My journey* page on the course site).
2. Open Claude, ChatGPT or any AI you use, paste in the prompt below, then paste your context where it says to.
3. Answer its questions. At the end it writes a draft artifact from your answers.
4. Edit it until it sounds like you, add any images or links, and submit **the artifact**. Including the conversation or a link to it is welcome but not required.

## 3. The AI interview prompt

**Gather your context first.** Your *My journey* page (https://cu.learnvibe.build/account/journey/) has every week's assignment and your own submissions on one page, with a button to copy them as markdown. Your posts and your pod's on the class board (https://cu.learnvibe.build/board/) are useful context too.

Then copy this prompt into your AI:

```
You're going to interview me about my final project for a course called Learn, Vibe, Build, where students build things with AI. Your job is to help me get clear on what I'm making and why, and then turn my answers into a short artifact I can share with my project pod. Don't plan the project for me, and don't suggest ideas unless I ask.

First, here is my context from the semester so far (earlier submissions, reflections, links). Read it, but don't summarise it back to me; use it to ask better questions:

[PASTE YOUR EARLIER SUBMISSIONS, REFLECTIONS OR PROJECT LINKS HERE — or write "none"]

How to run the interview:
- Ask ONE question at a time and wait for my answer.
- Start by asking what I'm thinking of working on, even if it's still vague. If my context suggests a direction, you can ask whether I want to grow something I've already started.
- Cover these, in whatever order follows from my answers:
  1. What exactly am I planning to make?
  2. Who is it for, and what does it make better for them?
  3. What will the finished version look like, and what happens when someone uses it?
  4. Why this project? Why does it matter to me?
  5. What skills do I want to grow?
  6. What have I already built or learned this semester that this grows from?
  7. What support do I need: skills I'm missing, questions I'm stuck on, the kind of people I'd like in my pod, and whether I'd rather build solo with pod support or build together?
  8. What's the smallest version that would already be worth showing someone?
- When an answer is vague, ask for an example or "what would that look like?" Push gently on who it's for and why.
- Keep it to about 10-15 questions. Then tell me you're ready to write it up.

Then write my artifact, in the first person, using only what I said (don't add anything I didn't say), with these headings:
- My project in one sentence
- What I'm making
- Who it's for and what it makes better
- What the finished version looks like
- Why I'm doing it
- Skills I'm growing
- Where I'm starting from
- Support I need (including solo vs. build together)
- Smallest version worth showing

Finally, ask me what I want to change before I submit it.
```
